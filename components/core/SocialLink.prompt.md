White social icon + label that reddens on hover; for footers and speaker cards.

```jsx
<SocialLink network="linkedin" label="LinkedIn" base="../../" />
<SocialLink network="instagram" label="Instagram" base="../../" />
<SocialLink network="linkedin" label="LinkedIn" size="sm" />
```

`network`: linkedin | instagram | youtube. `size`: md (default, footer) | sm (under a speaker portrait). Set `base` to reach the assets/ folder from the page's depth.
