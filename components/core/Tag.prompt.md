Uppercase eyebrow label above a heading, white by default ("Speakers", "About", "Topic"). With `dot` it becomes a meta line: a red dot before a name, date or venue, in regular weight and sentence case.

```jsx
<Tag>Start-up accelerator</Tag>
<Tag dot>24 June</Tag>
<Tag dot color="muted">Computational artist</Tag>
```

`color`: white (default) | red | muted. `dot` adds the leading red dot and switches to the meta style.
